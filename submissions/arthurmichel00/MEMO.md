# MIB Doc Challenge — Technical Memo

**GitHub:** arthurmichel00 · **Solution repo:** `https://github.com/arthurmichel00/mib-doc-solution` (MIT; PyMuPDF is AGPL-3.0 — see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
**Runtime:** Tesseract + a bundled RapidOCR PP-OCRv6 ONNX fallback + a measured preprocessing escalation + a strip-scoped fine-tuned Tesseract LSTM + OpenCV + deterministic rules. No LLMs/VLMs, no network, no per-case hardcoding.
**Process:** Built by AI agents in autonomous loops: every lever flag-gated and A/B-measured against gates evaluated in code. Humans made three kinds of call — goals, trust boundaries, ship decisions. Receipts: LEVERS.md and APPENDIX.md in the solution repo.

## Results

- **Official train: 129.14 / 150** on the full Docker scoring contract over all 1,000 cases — extraction 45.51 / 50, classification 66.67 / 80, calibration 16.96 / 20 at Brier 0.0760.
- **Held-out 200: 130.57 / 150** (Brier 0.0712), holdout ≥ train at every measured milestone; back-scored, the arc runs 113.19 → 130.57.
- **Catastrophic false approvals: 1**, the documented designed trap MIB-000865.
- **0 fallback rows**; every recovered adjudicator note adjudicates correctly (343/343; the one prior mis-bind is fixed — see Failure modes).
- **5,000 / 5,000 validation rows**, written by the frozen image, validated against the manifest.
- **5.87 s/PDF on a quiet box against the 6.0 budget, 0.36 GiB image** (including the 15 MB fine-tuned tessdata artifact). The previous build measured 5.81 and the one before it 5.64: the two reading channels added here cost ~0.06 s/PDF between them, on top of the candidate-scoring pass that costs ~0.3 s on each of the ~20% of cases that trigger it. We breached our own 5.6 target knowingly. 5.87 is the clean figure on the fixed 100-case timing subset; the same image reads the full 1,000-case corpus at 5.01 s/PDF end-to-end, and we quote the stricter number. We ship the points and disclose the cost.

## Why this number and not a bigger one

The challenge deliberately makes ~5–7% of cases under-determined: silent disqualifying flags with no readable evidence, whose train labels say DENIED. The organizers confirmed in issues #4/#5 that NEEDS_REVIEW is the correct output there and that systems "should not guess." We never do: absent evidence never feeds adjudication (unreadable *field text* is a different, extraction-only matter — see Disclosures). Beating ~132 on train takes surrendering that bucket to train-label expected value, or transcribing the hidden answer key — organizer-confirmed poison, its adjudication wrong in 216/216 carriers. Both inflate the public number, collapse on private labels, and carry disqualification risk. We stopped at the honest maximum and optimized for the private test; the holdout trajectory is the proof.

## How this was built — the operating model

One human directed this: I set the goals, drew the trust boundaries, and made the ship calls; gated agent loops did everything else. Verification was machinery, not meetings. Every lever was built behind a feature flag and A/B-measured on the full train set against gates evaluated *in code*: score ≥ baseline, holdout ≥ train, single designed-trap CFA, zero fallback rows, red-team green. Agents proceeded on green and killed on red unattended, in overnight stretches of 8–12 hours, and dead ends went to an append-only ledger with their evidence so later loops never re-litigated them. More than forty levers were measured this way; seventeen survived every gate to ship enabled.

## Approach

The pipeline is built around one idea: decide the trust boundary before reading anything.

1. **Redact-then-render.** Hidden spans (render-mode-3, opacity-0, sub-6pt, near-white fill, off-cropbox) are classified from the PDF appearance stream and redacted *before* rasterization, so no contrast step can resurrect a white-on-white injection. Non-footer text-layer content on scan pages is untrusted by construction; the hidden "answer key" is never read at runtime.
2. **OCR ensemble, adopted by measurement.** Scans render at 288 DPI into a pooled Tesseract ladder (binarization, deskew, orientation retries) pooling passes per line by confidence. A bundled RapidOCR PP-OCRv6 ONNX fallback fires only when a decision-relevant field is still unread (v6 measured +0.81 and shipped; v5 was a wash at +0.07). A final preprocessing escalation may only fill unread fields, never out-vote a read one. Behind it, for the four closed-menu fields only, a constrained-candidate channel scores every legal value against the recognizer's frame posteriors with the exact CTC forward algorithm instead of decoding its argmax — a mechanism from a MIT-licensed public solution, re-gated on our data and attributed in ATTRIBUTION.md. Two further channels sit beside it in this build: a generator-inversion reader that renders every legal candidate through a degradation kernel recovered from the damaged row itself and picks by pixel correlation, which reads rows where no decoder gets usable frames at all; and a likelihood-fusion layer that averages each candidate's log-likelihood across preprocessing views and across pages, so the channel decides once on all the evidence instead of once per view. A fine-tuned Tesseract LSTM from another public MIT solution (redistributed verbatim, sha-pinned, attributed) runs only at the escalation tier and only on already-located value strips; the page-level variant, at a measured 0.371 s/page, cost several times what the budget affords and was rejected in design.
3. **Extraction.** Label-anchored parsing, closed-vocabulary weighted-Levenshtein correction (with fusion/ligature edit costs and a licensed truncation-prefix rule for clipped rows), and precedence-weighted cross-page reconciliation where an exact digital line beats any OCR vote-sum, with a decoy-aware guard for names. Trusted note Reason lines are mined for field values, each template 100% gold-verified. A deterministic truth table resolves fee status across all 449 digital receipts.
4. **Adjudication.** A deterministic policy engine with **positive-evidence gates**. Deny rules fire only on affirmative reads; APPROVED requires flags, visa, world, sponsor (unless DIP-1), fee, and arrival each read affirmatively. Absent evidence is never clean evidence.
5. **Decision + calibration.** A per-path posterior feeds an expected-value argmax under the scorer's 8/−4/2/1 payoff. Confidence is each path's empirical accuracy with shrinkage (m=10), fit out-of-fold (seed-8090 800/200) and refit at freeze from in-container OCR.

An internal red-team broke the trusted Finding line four ways; all four are closed, and the attack corpus re-runs green after every change.

## Failure modes

- **One accepted catastrophic false approval, MIB-000865.** The scanned intake *visibly* prints "Visa Class: XW-2"; the truth is TRANSIT-7 (DENIED), and nothing in the packet contradicts it. The pixels lie by design. Guarding would mean distrusting every single-source scan read, converting ~45 legitimate approvals into reviews to save one −4. We took the loss.
- **One misread note, MIB-000497 — found post-freeze, fixed since.** A legible note reads "Finding: NEEDS_REVIEW"; damage truncates the line, and under the image's Tesseract 5.3 the fuzzy matcher mis-bound the fragment to DENIED. The fix is a truncation-ambiguity guard: the winner must also win a junk-stripped label-zone re-scoring, else the matcher abstains. It shipped after the full battery, including a 23,066-line matcher sweep with two changed lines, both benign.
- **Three over-emitted risk flags** (MIB-000111/000376/000452), the earlier clipped-flag repair's standing cost: each adds one flag token gold does not carry, on cases already denied by other flags. The same repair bought two correct NEEDS_REVIEW → DENIED adjudications.
- **Three field losses this build bought its 19 field gains with.** Two are previously-correct candidate fills the new acceptance layers no longer accept, so the field falls back to the corpus-mode default (MIB-000369 visa, MIB-000570 purpose); one is a new value landing where the mode default happened to be gold-correct (MIB-000032 purpose). No adjudication moved in either direction — the count of changed verdicts against the previous build is zero. Full flip audit in APPENDIX.md and LEVERS.md.

## What I would improve with another week

Each item below already has a measurement behind it, and ships when it earns its gates:

- **The next step on the reading channel is a measurement, not a plan.** Cross-view likelihood fusion — scoring each candidate under all the evidence at once rather than per view — shipped in this build. The structural change still outstanding is making the second engine primary and spending the freed average budget on full-page fine-tuned reading of the under-determined tail. Its payoff bound is already measured, and it is not the obvious one: the skippable cases are the cheap tail (all-read cases average 6.6 s of serial OCR against a 22.5 s corpus mean), so the swap has to pay for itself with what the freed budget buys, not with the saving. It goes into the next measurement slot against a hard gate — at least this build's score at ≤ 6.0 s/PDF clean, or it is dropped rather than shipped. A grid-path deskew retry that revives dead lattice fits rides the same slot. Behind both: the unresampled native-raster pass, and the slice-shift fragment repair whose frame-anchored gating our code audit validated as the answer to the safety objection that keeps general realignment out.
- **Make the note-rescue channel version-portable:** a measured one-line padding fix for the image's Tesseract clipping glyphs at the crop edge. Built, in the tree, default-off — the channel is redundant in-container, so the pad is private-set insurance that ships behind its own A/B.
- **Grow the red-team corpus** past the injection mechanisms observed in train (4 → 10 attacks during this build); crack **MIB-000538's undocumented second deny pattern** (unsolved field-wide); build the honest-nulls output mode.

Five I would not retry, all ledgered with their evidence: sponsor-digit shape recovery on degraded scans, measured at chance (every SPN-#### shares glyph geometry); vocabulary-constrained OCR user-words, net-negative at −0.22 and left dead in the tree; candidate scoring for arrival dates, which decodes the year *below* chance because 477 same-width candidates share ~90% of the canvas and the score spread falls an order of magnitude under the degradation noise; fee recovery on scanned receipts, where the generator prints the value away, so a perfect reader fills nothing; and a per-field margin floor on top of the candidate channel — endorsed by its own synthetic census, refuted by the corpus, since in composition it blocked one wrong fill at the cost of eight right ones and was dropped from the shipped arm on that measurement.

## Disclosures

- **Extraction-only imputation + emission-time consistency guard.** Unreadable fields print corpus-mode fallbacks for schema completeness — the scorer treats wrong and blank identically — and those values are quarantined from the decision layer, as are constrained-candidate fills, capped below the affirmative-read threshold so no policy rule can consume one. Where a trusted note decides a case, a contradicting low-trust read is replaced from that note's entailed set.
- **Prior-art overlap.** The fee geometry, the render-first trust boundary, the name syllable grammar, and the CTC candidate-scoring and fusion-cost mechanisms above circulate in public MIT-licensed solutions; we re-derived, re-gated, and extended each, with per-mechanism credit in ATTRIBUTION.md. Two components in this build are not re-derivations and are labelled as such: the generator-inversion reader is a **port** of another entrant's MIT-licensed modules (two of its constants re-measured on our corpus, a damage sentinel and a self-deskew pass added — the deskew plus a wider page budget took registration from 15/31 to 25/31 of the real slots), and the fine-tuned Tesseract LSTM is redistributed **verbatim** with its sha pinned in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. The likelihood fusion above is ours.
- **Learned entity lists, no per-case logic.** Our rule miner mined the policy table from train labels (1000/1000 given true fields); the revoked-sponsor list is 3 documented IDs plus 3 mined from train recurrence. Case ids appear only as evidence citations in comments — no id-keyed lookups, no allowlists, and a grep for case-id comparisons comes back empty.
- **Dev-time VLM cross-check, diagnostic only.** GPT and Gemini read rendered pages during development to locate our OCR failures. No VLM output entered predictions; the runtime is offline.
- **Third-party components.** RapidOCR, the bundled PP-OCR ONNX models, and Tesseract are Apache-2.0; onnxruntime is MIT; the bundled fine-tuned `mib.traineddata` artifact is MIT and credited to its author with its sha; PyMuPDF is AGPL-3.0 and governs the combined work; our code is MIT.
